NAC Catechism & Youth Policy / Phunziro ndi Ndondomeko

English & Chichewa Summary:

  • Article 1: I believe in God, the Father Almighty, Creator of heaven and earth. (Ndimakhulupirira mwa Mulungu, Atate Wamphamvuyonse, Mlengi wa kumwamba ndi dziko lapansi.)
  • Article 2: I believe in Jesus Christ, the only begotten Son of God, our Lord. (Ndimakhulupirira mwa Yesu Khristu, Mwana wobadwa yekha wa Mulungu, Ambuye wathu.)
  • Article 3: I believe in the Holy Spirit. (Ndimakhulupirira mwa Mzimu Woyera.)
  • Article 4: I believe that the Lord Jesus rules His Church and sends His Apostles. (Ndimakhulupirira kuti Ambuye Yesu amalamulira Mpingo wake ndi kutumiza Atumwi ake.)
  • Article 5: I believe that all ministries in the Church of Christ are chosen by Jesus Christ. (Ndimakhulupirira kuti unduna wonse mu Mpingo wa Khristu umasankhidwa ndi Yesu Khristu.)

Mu New Apostolic Church, tili ndi Zisakramenti zitatuzi zofunika kwambiri:

  1. Holy Baptism (Ubatizo Woyera): Chiyambi cha moyo watsopano mwa Khristu.
  2. Holy Communion (Mgonero Woyera): Kalandidwe ka Thupi ndi Magazi a Khristu pa gome loyera.
  3. Holy Sealing (Usindikizo Woyera): Kutsanuliridwa kwa Mzimu Woyera mwa kuikidwa manja ndi Mtumwi.

Cholinga cha Achinyamata a NAC:

  • Spiritual Growth: Kukula m'chikhulupiriro ndi m’mapemphero tsiku ndi tsiku.
  • Active Fellowship: Kutenga mbali mu Kwaya, Utumiki, ndi Zochitika za Achinyamata.
  • Christian Ethics: Kukhala chitsanzo chabwino mu chikhalidwe cha tsiku ndi tsiku m'gulu la anthu.
  • Church & Community Support: Kuthandiza amasiye, okalamba, ndi osowa m'mampingo 15 a Dedza East.
